Dyslexify

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 September 2026 · Last updated: 3 September 2026

Dyslexify is a reading app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, published by Juan Carlos Estévez Rodríguez (JCTec).

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Dyslexify.

The short version. Nothing you read reaches us, because there is nothing in the app that sends it. Dyslexify has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you read stays on your device and in your own private iCloud.

1.Information we collect

None.

Dyslexify has no account system, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. We do not collect, receive, buy, sell, or have access to any information about you, your device, or what you read.

We do not know how many documents you have, what is in them, how long you read, or whether you opened the app at all.

2.Information stored on your device

Everything you put into Dyslexify stays on your device, stored with Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • The documents you import, and the text extracted from them
  • Their titles, word counts, and when you added or last opened them
  • Your reading position in each document
  • Your typography settings — typeface, size, spacing, line width, page colour, read-aloud speed and voice

This data belongs to you. It is not transmitted to us and is not accessible to us.

3.iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive enabled, Dyslexify syncs the data listed above across your own Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit, in your private database.

That means it is stored in your personal iCloud account, under your Apple Account, subject to Apple's privacy policy and Apple's security. JCTec has no CloudKit public database, runs no server, and cannot see, query, or retrieve your private database. Neither can anyone else.

Sync is optional. You can turn iCloud off for Dyslexify in your device's system settings, and the app continues to work entirely locally.

4.Files you import

When you import a file, Dyslexify reads it once and copies the text into its own storage. It does not keep access to the original file, does not upload it anywhere, does not index your file system, and does not read anything you have not explicitly chosen through the system file picker.

5.Read-aloud

Read-aloud uses Apple's on-device speech synthesis. The text is spoken locally. It is not sent to us or to any third party.

Some system voices are downloaded from Apple by the operating system, on your instruction, outside of Dyslexify. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

6.Network use

Dyslexify makes no network requests of its own. The only network traffic associated with the app is Apple's iCloud sync, performed by the operating system, and only when you have iCloud enabled.

7.Children's privacy

Dyslexify collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 — or the equivalent minimum age in your country. Because nothing is collected, nothing about a child is collected either.

8.Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data a company holds about you.

We hold none. There is no account to close, no profile to export, and no record of you to delete, because none was ever created.

Data stored in your private iCloud database is under your control through your Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy governs it, and Apple's tools let you manage or delete it.

9.Retention and deletion

  • Deleting a document in the app removes it from that device, and from your other devices if iCloud sync is on.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its local data from that device.
  • iCloud data can be removed in your device's system settings, under iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

10.Typefaces

Dyslexify bundles the OpenDyslexic and Atkinson Hyperlegible typefaces under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. They are included in the app and load from it. No font is fetched over the network, and no font project receives any information about your use of the app.

11.Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above will change and the change will be noted in the app's release notes. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
