Tapper Counters

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 10, 2026

Tapper Counters is designed to help you create and manage simple counters on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, uses, and stores.

Information We Collect

Tapper Counters does not collect personal information. The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information.

Data Stored on Your Device

Counters you create, including counter names, values, increments, colors, and settings, are stored locally on your device so the app can display and manage them. This data is controlled by you and can be edited, reset, or deleted inside the app.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Tapper Counters does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Because the app does not collect personal information, there is no personal information to disclose to advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.

iCloud, Sync, and Notifications

The current version of Tapper Counters is intended to work locally on your device. If a future version adds iCloud sync, notifications, or other connected features, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released.

Children's Privacy

Tapper Counters does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention and Deletion

Counter data remains on your device until you edit it, reset it, delete it, or remove the app. Deleting the app removes the app's locally stored data from your device according to iOS behavior.

Security

Tapper Counters relies on Apple's built-in iOS security and app sandboxing to protect app data stored on your device. The app is designed to avoid collecting personal data in the first place.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or data practices change. Updates will be reflected by changing the effective date above.
